About

Fine jewelry, made to last a lifetime

Ranisa is a Fifth Avenue jeweler specializing in engagement rings and wedding bands. We help couples choose between lab-grown and natural diamonds, weigh cut, color, clarity and carat, and settle on a design that fits the way you live.

Every ring is made to order. Design one entirely your own with our configurator, or start from a piece in our lab-grown or natural diamond collections and adjust it until it is right.

Our approach

We source both lab-grown and natural stones and are glad to walk you through the trade-offs of each. Loose diamonds are accompanied by an independent grading report, and every finished ring is inspected before it leaves our hands.

We stand behind what we sell. Clients receive complimentary cleaning and inspection for the life of the ring, and we handle resizing and repairs in-house.
